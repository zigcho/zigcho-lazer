# score follows the settings now

DA no longer cuts your score just because you changed a value. tighter circles and hit windows raise it; easier settings lower it. the amount depends on the original map and what you changed, with HP carrying much less weight. unchanged DA is neutral. this applies across osu, taiko, catch and mania with each mode's relevant settings.

fixed the other obvious multiplier traps too: slightly faster DT/NC losing score, near-normal HT/DC taking a huge cut, flat penalties on Wind Up/Down, and custom Flashlight losing its bonus. Approach Different is neutral now, and key-count mods no longer take 10% off native mania maps where they have no effect.

this is actual score only. pp is unchanged. old scores and replays keep their original scoring rules. [the exact policy](../docs/score-multipliers.md).

windows x64, macos arm64, linux x64 and a signed android arm64 apk. the version footer includes our build number, and the GitHub builds are labelled with the version and build number too. ios still isn't in this release.
